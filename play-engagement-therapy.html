Play & Engagement Therapy

Uses play-based activities to encourage communication, social interaction and learning.

How it helps

  • Better communication and shared attention
  • Stronger social interaction
  • Healthy emotional expression
  • More active participation in learning

What a session may include

  • Child-led play with gentle guidance from the therapist
  • Turn-taking and cooperative games
  • Pretend play and storytelling
  • Parent tips to continue play-based learning at home

Who it may help

Children who find it hard to engage, play with others or stay involved in activities.

Every programme is tailored to your child. The first visit is an assessment, and Dr. Devyani will explain the plan before therapy begins.
